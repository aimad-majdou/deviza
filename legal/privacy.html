Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

The Service generates quotes, invoices, delivery notes and purchase orders for small businesses from messages sent over WhatsApp. This policy describes what we hold, why, and for how long.

Data controller: Aimad MAJDOU Contact: devisbot.ma@gmail.com

What we collect

From you, when you set up your profile

  • Your company name, address and city
  • Your ICE, IF and RC numbers — Moroccan tax and registration identifiers, required by Article 145 of the CGI to appear on a valid invoice
  • Your logo and stamp images, if you send them
  • Your WhatsApp phone number

From your messages

  • The text of messages you send us, so line items can be extracted from them
  • The documents produced: line descriptions, quantities, prices and totals
  • Where a message names your customer, that customer's name, and their ICE, address and city when given — a B2B invoice is not legally valid without identifying the purchaser
  • Images you send as proof of payment

Automatically

  • When each message and document was created
  • Which documents were generated, and how many, for your usage limits

We do not collect your WhatsApp contacts, your location, or any message not sent to our number.

Automated processing

Message text is sent to a third-party automated language-processing service to extract line items. That processing produces the contents of your document. It is not used to make decisions about you, and we do not use it for profiling or advertising.

Your messages are not used to train the provider's models, and are not read by human reviewers. We use a paid processing tier specifically to obtain that guarantee; the free tiers of these services generally reserve the right to do both. The provider retains message text only briefly, to detect abuse of its own service.

You may ask us which provider this is at any time, and we will tell you. We name it on request rather than on this page so that we can change provider without the page going out of date — the safeguards described below apply whichever one we use.

Who else sees it

  • Meta (WhatsApp) — delivers the messages, and is subject to its own terms
  • Our language-processing provider — receives message text for extraction, as above
  • Our hosting provider — stores the database

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Data is stored on servers in the European Union. Under Article 43 of Law 09-08 this is a transfer abroad, and it is declared to the CNDP as such. Morocco does not maintain a list of countries with recognised adequate protection, so this rests on the safeguards the destination applies rather than on any automatic adequacy — EU providers are subject to the GDPR, which exceeds the protection Law 09-08 requires.

We do not store data in the United States.

How long we keep it

  • Invoices and their records: 10 years. Moroccan law requires this, and we cannot delete them earlier even at your request.
  • Quotes, delivery notes and purchase orders: while your account is active, then 12 months.
  • Payment proofs: until your subscription is activated, then 12 months.
  • Message text: as part of the document it produced.

Invoices are stored with a cryptographic hash chain so that any alteration is detectable. That protects you as much as us: it is what lets an auditor establish a document has not been changed since it was issued.

Your rights

Under Law 09-08 you may ask us to:

  • see what we hold about you
  • correct anything wrong — you can also do this yourself with the modifier command
  • delete your data, except invoices inside their legal retention period
  • object to processing, which in practice means closing your account

Write to the contact address at the top of this page. We will respond within 30 days.

You may also complain to the CNDP (Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel), which supervises data protection in Morocco.

Security

Access to the admin panel requires authentication. Portal links are signed, expire, and are bound to the device that requested them. Inbound WhatsApp messages are cryptographically verified as genuinely from Meta before being processed. The database is backed up on a schedule.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you.

Children

The Service is for registered businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will say so on this page and update the date above.
